Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Looper, a music creation app published by Hashir, handles your information.

What Looper stores and why

  • Recordings and imported files. Looper uses audio, video, and WAV files you record or select to create, edit, play back, and export your projects. A recording can contain the voice or image of anyone captured in it.
  • Projects and exports. Looper saves session names, track settings, beat patterns, waveforms, and media files so you can reopen your work. It stores WAV and video exports so you can access or share them later.
  • Preferences. Looper remembers settings such as haptic feedback and whether you have completed onboarding.

Looper processes and stores this information on your device. It runs without an account and has no built-in cloud sync, analytics, or advertising trackers. We do not sell your personal information.

Microphone, camera, and files

Looper asks for microphone access when you use an audio recording feature. A Cappella uses the camera for preview and video capture, and uses the microphone to include sound in the take. The system file picker gives Looper access to the files you choose to import. You can revoke microphone or camera permissions in your device settings. Revoking a permission leaves any saved recordings in place.

Sharing and copies

When you choose Share, Looper passes the selected file to your device’s share sheet. You choose where it goes. Any destination you select handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Your device’s backup settings may also affect whether copies of app data are included in device backups.

Retention and deletion

Looper keeps local projects, media, and exports so they remain available when you reopen the app. Deleting a session removes its entry from Library. Recordings, imported copies, and exports may remain in app storage. To remove app storage, clear Looper’s storage in Android settings or delete the app on iOS. If you use the browser version, clear the site data in your browser settings. Copies you shared, downloaded, or backed up must be managed separately.
